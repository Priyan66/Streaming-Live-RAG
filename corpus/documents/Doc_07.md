# Doc_07: Booking Exceptions and Late Bookings

## §1 Booking Channel
All flights and hotels for business travel must be booked through the corporate travel desk using the TravelDesk portal. Bookings made through the travel desk are billed directly to the company and do not require the employee to pay upfront.

## §2 Late and Post-Travel Bookings
A booking requested less than seven days before departure requires a written justification from the department head. A booking made after the travel has already taken place, such as reimbursing a ticket the employee bought during the trip, is treated as a post-travel booking exception. Post-travel booking exceptions require approval from a Senior Director before any reimbursement is processed.

## §3 Self-Booked Travel
If an employee books travel outside the travel desk without an approved exception, reimbursement is capped at the fare the travel desk quoted for the same route and date. The employee bears any difference in cost.

## §4 Emergency Travel
In a genuine emergency, an employee may book travel directly and notify the travel desk within 24 hours. Emergency bookings are reimbursed in full once the reporting manager confirms the emergency in writing.
