# Doc_05: Travel Reimbursement Policy — Standard Rules

## §1 Scope and Eligibility
This policy applies to all full-time and fixed-term employees of Kestrel Systems who travel on an approved business trip. Interns may claim travel expenses only when the trip is pre-approved by their programme coordinator. Contractors are reimbursed under the terms of their individual contracts and are not covered by this policy.

## §2 Standard Reimbursement Rule
Employees are reimbursed for actual and reasonable business expenses incurred during an approved trip. Airfare is reimbursed at economy class for flights shorter than six hours. Every claim must be supported by itemised receipts and filed within 30 days of the trip ending. The employee's reporting manager must approve the claim before Finance releases payment.

## §3 Non-Reimbursable Expenses
The company does not reimburse personal entertainment, minibar charges, traffic or parking fines, seat or cabin upgrades purchased for comfort, laundry for trips shorter than four nights, or expenses incurred by accompanying family members. Loyalty points earned on business travel remain with the employee but cannot be exchanged for cash reimbursement.

## §4 Travel Advances
An employee may request a travel advance of up to 60 percent of the estimated trip cost through the finance portal. The advance must be settled against the final expense claim within 30 days of returning. Any unspent advance must be returned to the company along with the claim.
