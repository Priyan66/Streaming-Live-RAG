# Doc_25: Leave Policy

## §1 Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued at two days per month. Leave requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three days.

## §2 Sick Leave
Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than two consecutive days.

## §3 Carry Forward
Up to 10 days of unused annual leave may be carried forward to the next year. Carried-forward leave that is not used by 31 March of the following year lapses.

## §4 Leave Combined with Business Travel
Employees may add personal leave days to a business trip with manager approval. Any additional hotel nights, meals or changes to flights caused by the personal days are paid by the employee.
