# Doc_35: IT Helpdesk and Laptop Support

## §1 Raising an IT Ticket
IT issues are reported through the helpdesk portal or by calling the helpdesk extension 4357. Priority 1 tickets, such as a complete loss of access, receive a response within 30 minutes.

## §2 Laptop Repair and Replacement
If a company laptop fails and cannot be repaired on the same day, IT provides a loaner laptop. A permanent replacement is issued within three working days when the original device cannot be repaired.

## §3 Lost or Stolen Devices
A lost or stolen laptop or phone must be reported to IT within 24 hours so that the device can be remotely locked and wiped. A police report is required for stolen devices.
