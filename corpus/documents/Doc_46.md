# Doc_46: Attendee Data Privacy for Events

## §1 Collecting Attendee Data
Organisers should collect only the attendee information needed to run the event, such as name, organisation, email address and dietary requirements. Sensitive personal data must not be collected on registration forms.

## §2 Sharing Data with Venues
Only the attendee count and dietary counts may be shared with venues and caterers. Full attendee lists may be shared with a venue only when the venue requires them for building security.

## §3 Retention and Deletion
Attendee data must be deleted within 90 days after the event unless the attendee has agreed to receive future communication. Deletion is confirmed by the event organiser in the events tracker.
