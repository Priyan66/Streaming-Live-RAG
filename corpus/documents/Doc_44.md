# Doc_44: Ground Transport and Airport Transfers

## §1 Local Transport
Local travel during business trips should use the company's approved ride-hailing account, which is billed directly to the company. Auto-rickshaw and taxi fares paid in cash are reimbursed against a written log of trips.

## §2 Airport Transfers
Airport transfers are reimbursed at actual cost for rides to and from the airport at the start and end of a business trip. Premium car categories are not reimbursed unless no standard vehicle is available.

## §3 Use of a Personal Vehicle
Employees who use their own car for business travel are reimbursed at INR 12 per kilometre, and two-wheelers at INR 6 per kilometre, based on a mileage log. Tolls and parking are reimbursed at actual cost.
