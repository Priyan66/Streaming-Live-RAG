# Doc_06: International Travel Addendum

## §1 Applicability
This addendum applies in addition to the standard reimbursement rules whenever a business trip crosses a national border. Where this addendum and the standard rules differ, the addendum takes precedence for the international portion of the trip.

## §2 Foreign Currency Receipts
Expenses paid in a foreign currency must be supported by the original receipt and a currency conversion record, such as a card statement or a bank forex slip. Finance verifies every foreign currency receipt against the reference exchange rate on the transaction date before approving reimbursement. Receipts that are not in English must include a short translated summary written by the employee.

## §3 International Meal Allowance
Meals on international trips are covered by a daily allowance of USD 75 in standard cities and USD 95 in high-cost cities such as London, New York, Tokyo and Singapore. The allowance is paid in Indian rupees at the reference exchange rate on the first day of travel.

## §4 International Pre-Trip Approval
International trips must be approved by a Vice President at least 21 days before departure. The approval request must include the business purpose, the itinerary and an estimated budget.
