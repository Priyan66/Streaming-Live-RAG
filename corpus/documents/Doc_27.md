# Doc_27: Remote Work and Home Office Equipment

## §1 Remote Work Eligibility
Employees who have completed their probation period may work remotely up to three days a week with their manager's agreement. Roles that require on-site lab access are excluded.

## §2 Equipment Provided
Remote workers receive a company laptop, one external monitor, a keyboard and a headset. All equipment remains company property and must be returned when the employee leaves.

## §3 Home Office Stipend
Eligible remote workers receive a home office stipend of INR 1,500 per month to cover internet and electricity costs. The stipend is paid with the monthly salary and does not require receipts.
