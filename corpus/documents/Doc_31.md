# Doc_31: Venue Booking Cancellation and Refund Policy

## §1 Booking Deposits
Venue bookings for workshops and events are confirmed only after a deposit of 30 percent of the total venue cost is paid. The balance is due seven days before the event date.

## §2 Rescheduling
Each venue booking may be rescheduled once without charge if the request is made at least 21 days before the original event date. Later rescheduling requests are treated as a cancellation followed by a new booking.

## §3 Force Majeure
If an event cannot take place because of a natural disaster, government order or a similar event beyond anyone's control, the full deposit is refunded or carried over to a new date within 12 months.

## §4 Standard Cancellation Terms
If a venue booking is cancelled 30 or more days before the event, the deposit is refunded in full minus a processing fee of INR 2,000. Cancellations made 15 to 29 days before the event forfeit 50 percent of the deposit. Cancellations made less than 15 days before the event forfeit the entire deposit and 25 percent of the remaining balance.
