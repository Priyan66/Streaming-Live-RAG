# Doc_42: Event Planning Timeline and Approvals

## §1 Planning Lead Times
Workshops for more than 25 attendees should be planned at least six weeks in advance so that venue, catering and AV bookings can be secured. Smaller sessions need at least two weeks of lead time.

## §2 Event Approvals
Events that involve external customers require approval from the marketing team. Any event with a total budget above INR 2,00,000 also requires approval from the Finance business partner before bookings are confirmed.

## §3 Attendee Communication
Invitations should be sent at least three weeks before the event, with a reminder one week before. The invitation must state the venue address, start time and any dietary information requested from attendees.
