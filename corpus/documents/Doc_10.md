# Doc_10: Domestic Per Diem Rates

## §1 Tier 1 City Per Diem
Employees travelling on business to Tier 1 cities receive a per diem of INR 2,000 per day for meals and incidental expenses. Receipts are not required for amounts within the per diem.

## §2 Tier 2 and Other Cities
The per diem for Tier 2 cities and all other domestic locations is INR 1,400 per day. Where a client or the company provides meals, the per diem is reduced by INR 400 for each meal provided.

## §3 Partial Travel Days
On departure and return days, employees receive 50 percent of the applicable per diem if they are away for less than 12 hours on that day. Day trips that do not include an overnight stay are paid at 50 percent of the per diem.
