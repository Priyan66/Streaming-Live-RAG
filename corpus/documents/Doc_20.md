# Doc_20: Corporate Card Policy

## §1 Eligibility
Corporate cards are issued to employees who travel on business more than four times a year or who manage event budgets. Requests are approved by the department head and processed by Finance within two weeks.

## §2 Permitted Use
The corporate card may be used only for business expenses such as travel, accommodation, client meals and event costs. Cash withdrawals are not permitted except during international travel, where withdrawals of up to USD 200 per trip are allowed.

## §3 Monthly Reconciliation
Cardholders must reconcile every card transaction in ExpenseHub by the fifth working day of the following month. Unreconciled transactions older than 60 days are recovered from the employee's salary.

## §4 Lost or Stolen Cards
A lost or stolen corporate card must be reported to the card issuer and to Finance within 24 hours. The employee is not liable for fraudulent transactions reported within this window.
