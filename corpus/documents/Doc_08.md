# Doc_08: Hotel and Accommodation Policy

## §1 Domestic Hotel Limits
Hotel stays in Tier 1 cities are reimbursed up to INR 9,000 per night, and stays in Tier 2 cities up to INR 6,000 per night, including taxes. Tier 1 cities are Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Pune, Chennai and Hyderabad.

## §2 International Hotel Limits
Hotel stays abroad are reimbursed up to USD 220 per night in standard cities and USD 320 per night in high-cost cities. Stays above the limit require Vice President approval before the booking is made.

## §3 Extended Stays
For assignments longer than seven consecutive nights in one city, employees should use a serviced apartment arranged by the travel desk, which is usually cheaper than a hotel for long stays.

## §4 Hotel Cancellations and No-Shows
Employees must cancel unused hotel nights through the travel desk before the hotel's cancellation deadline. No-show charges caused by the employee failing to cancel are deducted from the expense claim unless the reporting manager confirms the trip was cancelled for business reasons.
