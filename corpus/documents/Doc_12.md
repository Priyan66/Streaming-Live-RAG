# Doc_12: Pune Workshop Venues

## §1 Approved Venues in Pune
Kestrel Systems has three approved venues in Pune for customer workshops and training sessions: Orchid Hall in Koregaon Park, the Deccan Conference Centre in Hinjewadi and Riverside Studio in Kalyani Nagar. Bookings for all three are made through the events team.

## §2 Orchid Hall
Orchid Hall seats up to 40 people in a classroom layout and up to 60 people in a theatre layout. The full-day rate is INR 45,000 and includes a projector, two wireless microphones and high-speed Wi-Fi. In-house catering is mandatory at Orchid Hall.

## §3 Deccan Conference Centre
The Deccan Conference Centre has meeting halls for groups of 25 to 150 people. Hall B is suited to groups of about 30 people and costs INR 38,000 per day. Larger halls are priced on request and must be booked at least eight weeks in advance.

## §4 Riverside Studio
Riverside Studio is an informal workspace that accommodates up to 20 people. It costs INR 18,000 per day and does not include AV equipment. Riverside Studio is the only Pune venue that allows external caterers.
