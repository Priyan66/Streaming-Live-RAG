# Doc_33: Meeting Room Displays and Office Electronics — Warranty and Repair

## §1 Warranty Coverage
Meeting room televisions and display panels are covered by a two-year manufacturer warranty on the panel and a one-year warranty on remotes and wall mounts. The warranty covers manufacturing defects but not physical or liquid damage.

## §2 Troubleshooting a Display with No Picture
If a television or display shows no picture, first check that the power cable and HDMI cable are firmly connected and that the correct input source is selected. Holding the power button on the panel for 10 seconds performs a soft reset that clears most startup faults.

## §3 Raising a Repair Request
If troubleshooting does not fix the fault, raise a repair request on the facilities portal with the asset tag, the serial number and a photo of the fault. A technician visits within 48 hours in metro offices and within five working days elsewhere.

## §4 Replacement Units
If a repair will take longer than five working days, facilities installs a temporary replacement display so that the meeting room remains usable.
