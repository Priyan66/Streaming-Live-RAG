# Doc_22: Visa and Travel Insurance

## §1 Visa Support
The travel desk provides invitation and support letters for business visas. Employees should apply for a visa at least 30 days before departure, and the visa fee is reimbursed as a business expense.

## §2 Travel Insurance
Travel insurance is mandatory for all international business trips and is provided under the company's group travel policy at no cost to the employee. The policy covers medical emergencies up to USD 100,000, trip cancellation and lost baggage.

## §3 Medical Emergencies Abroad
In a medical emergency abroad, employees should contact the insurer's 24-hour assistance line printed on the insurance card. Cashless treatment is available at network hospitals, and other medical expenses are reimbursed by the insurer on submission of bills.
