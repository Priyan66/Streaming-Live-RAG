# Doc_09: Catering Services for Events

## §1 On-Site Catering Options
Orchid Hall and the Deccan Conference Centre require event organisers to use their in-house catering. Both venues offer a Standard lunch package at INR 650 per person and a Premium lunch package at INR 950 per person. Tea and coffee breaks cost INR 180 per person per break.

## §2 External Caterers
External caterers are permitted only at Riverside Studio in Pune and at the Andheri Tech Hub in Mumbai. An external caterer must be on the company's approved vendor list and must hold a valid FSSAI food safety licence.

## §3 Dietary Requirements
Vegetarian, vegan, Jain and gluten-free meals are available at every approved venue. Dietary counts must be confirmed with the caterer at least five working days before the event, and late changes may not be accommodated.

## §4 Minimum Orders and Final Numbers
In-house catering requires a minimum order of 20 people per meal. Final attendee numbers must be confirmed 72 hours before the event, and the organiser is charged for the confirmed number even if fewer people attend.
