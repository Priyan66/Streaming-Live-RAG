# Doc_17: Expense Claim Submission Process

## §1 Submitting a Claim
All expense claims are submitted through the ExpenseHub portal. Each claim must list every expense line separately and attach a scanned copy of the matching receipt.

## §2 Submission Deadlines
Claims should be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred. Claims submitted between 31 and 90 days late need an explanation approved by the reporting manager, and claims older than 90 days are rejected automatically.

## §3 Receipt Standards
Receipts must be itemised and legible and must show the vendor name, date and amount. For any single expense above INR 5,000, a GST invoice in the company's name is required instead of a simple receipt.

## §4 Reimbursement Timeline
Approved claims are paid into the employee's salary account within 10 working days of final approval. Employees can track the status of each claim on the ExpenseHub dashboard.
