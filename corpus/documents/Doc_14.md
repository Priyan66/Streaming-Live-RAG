# Doc_14: Mumbai Workshop Venues

## §1 Approved Venues in Mumbai
Kestrel Systems has two approved venues in Mumbai for customer workshops: the Harbour View Room at Nariman Point and the Andheri Tech Hub. Bookings are made through the events team at least four weeks in advance.

## §2 Harbour View Room
The Harbour View Room seats up to 50 people and costs INR 70,000 per day. The rate includes AV equipment, a dedicated event coordinator and in-house catering service.

## §3 Andheri Tech Hub
The Andheri Tech Hub accommodates up to 30 people in a boardroom or classroom layout and costs INR 40,000 per day. The Andheri Tech Hub permits external caterers from the approved vendor list.
