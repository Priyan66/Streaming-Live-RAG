# Doc_40: AV Equipment and Event Technology

## §1 Standard AV Kit
The events team can provide a standard AV kit for venues without built-in equipment. The kit includes a portable projector, a 100-inch screen, two wireless microphones and a speaker system.

## §2 AV Rental Charges
The standard AV kit costs INR 6,500 per day, including delivery and setup. A technician to operate the equipment throughout the event costs an additional INR 4,000 per day.

## §3 Hybrid and Streamed Events
For hybrid workshops, the events team can set up a conference camera and a streaming laptop for up to 100 remote participants. Hybrid setups must be requested at least 10 working days before the event.
